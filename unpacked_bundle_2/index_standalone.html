ABOUT THE TEMPLE

A century of devotion in the heart of Telangana

అమ్మవారి ఆశీస్సులతో, ఊరి ప్రజల సేవలో

Established by village elders over four generations ago, the temple of Sri Bhavani Amma Vari has been the spiritual heart of our community. The annual Jathara draws thousands of devotees from surrounding mandals.

The temple is managed entirely by a volunteer committee. Our mission is simple: serve Amma Vari, serve the village, and account for every rupee with complete transparency.
